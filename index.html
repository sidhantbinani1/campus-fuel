From “what’s for lunch?” to a clearer choice.

02 / COMPARE

Find a meal that fits

Review listed calories, macros, and serving details. Missing values stay unknown. Published menus may change.

Order through your campus provider

Open the configured ordering destination. Students may need to sign in and select the meal again. No cart transfer is included.

A few things to know.

Does University Plates replace Grubhub?

No. The proposed handoff opens your campus’s configured ordering provider. Ordering, payment, availability, and checkout remain with that provider.

Can every school use the same features today?

No. Cal Poly SLO is the only campus with menu data in the current prototype. University-specific portals, provider links, and expanded coverage require setup.

Are university partnerships already in place?

No university partnerships or permissions to use university identities have been secured. This site invites those conversations.

How does dietary information work?

Available records can help inform a choice. Missing allergen information does not mean a meal is safe. Students should confirm ingredients and preparation concerns with campus dining staff.
