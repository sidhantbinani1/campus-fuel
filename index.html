The need is already part of student routine.

University Plates is a decision tool—not an ordering system. It helps students decide what fits before they eat, using available campus menu data and their own goals.

A few things to know.

Does University Plates handle meal ordering?

No. University Plates is a decision tool: it helps students understand choices before they eat. Current ordering, payment, availability, and checkout stay with campus dining workflows.

Can every school use the same features today?

No. Cal Poly SLO is the only campus with menu data in the current prototype. University-specific portals and expanded coverage require setup.

Are university partnerships already in place?

No university partnerships or permissions to use university identities have been secured. This site invites those conversations.

How does dietary information work?

Available records can help inform a choice. Missing allergen information does not mean a meal is safe. Students should confirm ingredients and preparation concerns with campus dining staff.
